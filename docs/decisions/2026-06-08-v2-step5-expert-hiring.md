## How it was built (the process record)

The productive-minding pattern: the two example hired experts were generated by a
minded background workflow (the way Eidolon hires at runtime, one agent per
expert), while the two procedure docs and the SKILL.md wiring were written inline
with liveness checks against the workflow transcript between each. The workflow was
block-waited at the dependency point and completed (two agents, about six minutes).
Claude stayed the sole filesystem writer.
