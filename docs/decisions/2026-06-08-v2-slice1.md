# Decision log: 2026-06-08 v2 slice 1 (persona system + engineering swarm)

Append-only. Records the first v2 increment and its canary, built directly inline
after two background design/verification workflows stalled on this machine (a
known pattern; the work was done by hand with focused subagents where independence
mattered).

## Context

v1 shipped and was canary-proven (commit 1f79374). v2 slice 1 was frozen in
`docs/spec/2026-06-08-eidolon-v2-scope.md`: the persona system plus the
engineering swarm, the smallest increment that proves Eidolon can staff a swarm
from a reusable template and have it do disciplined, self-bounded work. The user
approved the slice boundary before building.

## What was built

| Artifact | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `references/persona-template.md` | The ten-part construction template, the two anti-behavior layers (shared destructive floor + persona-specific), the anti-synthetic rail, and the persona file format. |
| `references/personas/fullstack-engineer.md` | One persona built from the template, all ten parts, proving the template produces a fully formed bounded persona. |
| `references/engineering-swarm.md` | The engineering swarm: builds against the spec, TDD per task, with the seeded-failing-test fire drill and the no-stub closeout gate. |
| `hooks/persona-conduct-guard.mjs` | Reads the seated persona (`.claude/active-persona.json`) and halts an action that crosses its declared anti-behaviors, naming the persona and the line. |
| `SKILL.md`, `.claude/settings.json`, `hooks/README.md`, `.gitignore` | BUILD wired to dispatch the swarm and seat the persona; the conduct guard wired into both matchers; the seat file git-ignored. |
| `scripts/persona-lint.mjs` + `.test.mjs` | The canary deliverable: validates a persona against the template and enforces the anti-synthetic rail (no anchor, no seat). |

## The canary (how the spine behaved)

- **Test-first proven.** The engineering swarm wrote `persona-lint.test.mjs` first
  and ran it before the implementation existed: it failed with
  `ERR_MODULE_NOT_FOUND` (the seeded-failing-test fire drill). Then the real
  implementation was written and the suite went green (2 tests, 2 pass, 0 fail).
- **The conduct guard halted a seeded anti-behavior at the right file:line.** With
  the full-stack engineer seated, an attempted stub
  (`throw new Error("not implemented yet")`) was blocked at
  `scripts/persona-lint.mjs:2`, naming the persona and `ship-stub-as-done`. The
  real implementation, with no stub, passed.
- **The anti-synthetic rail works end to end.** `persona-lint` validates the
  full-stack engineer persona (PASS) and rejects an anchorless ghost persona,
  naming the missing anchor (FAIL). No anchor, no seat.
- **No regression.** The v1 `selfcheck` still passes (hooks parse, settings valid,
  no em or en dashes); all v2 files are dash-clean and ASCII where required.

## Disposition

Slice 1 is accepted. Every "done when" criterion in the v2 scope met: a spec
produced, the engineering swarm built it test-first, the fire drill confirmed
test-first, the conduct guard halted a seeded violation at the right `file:line`,
and Close commits with this entry. The seeded stub lived only in a piped hook test
and never reached a commit.

## Decision

Proceed to v2 step 2 work on a spine that now staffs swarms with bounded personas.
Next deferred steps (in order): the other engineering personas and review swarms
(trust-and-safety, code-review) with the architect synthesis; then ship-and-upload;
then expert hiring and find-skills; then the full antibehavior catalog and the rest
of the hook suite.
